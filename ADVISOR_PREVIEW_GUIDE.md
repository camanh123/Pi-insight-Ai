# Pi Insight Advisor - Preview & User Guide

## What Changed
The AI Advisor in Pi Insight is now a true teaching expert. It doesn't just answer your questions—it educates you about Pi Network with structured, comprehensive responses backed by official Pi sources.

## Try These Questions First

### Question 1: "What is the Open Network?"
**Expected Response Structure:**
```
→ Direct Answer:
(Quick, 1-2 sentence explanation)

→ For Beginners:
(Simple explanation anyone can understand)

→ Why It Matters:
(Impact on the Pi community)

→ Real-Life Analogy:
(Example comparing to everyday concept)

→ Related Official Updates:
(List of related Pi updates)

→ Suggested Follow-Ups:
(2-3 questions to explore next)

→ Source & Date:
(Which official source + publication date)
```

### Question 2: "Explain KYC for someone new to Pi"
- You'll see a beginner-focused explanation
- Real-life analogy explaining identity verification
- Clear why-it-matters section
- Recommended follow-up: Learn about KYB

### Question 3: "What makes Nodes important?"
- Technical concept explained with analogies
- Short-term and long-term impact breakdown
- Links to related official announcements
- Suggestion to explore the Roadmap next

## Key Features

### 🎯 Smart Topic Detection
- Ask about anything Pi-related
- Advisor automatically detects the topic (Mainnet, KYC, KYB, Nodes, App Studio, Roadmap, Ecosystem)
- Learns from your questions to suggest next topics

### 📚 Teaching Structure
Every answer follows this proven learning pattern:
1. **Direct Answer** — Tell me now in 2 sentences
2. **Explain It** — I'm new to Pi, help me understand
3. **Why?** — What's the impact on me?
4. **Compare It** — Show me a real-world example
5. **Official Sources** — Where did this come from?
6. **What's Next?** — What should I explore next?

### ✅ Verified Information
- ✓ All answers come from official Pi sources only
- ✓ No guessing or speculation about unknown topics
- ✓ Clear distinction between facts and analysis
- ✓ Every answer cites its source and date

### 🔗 Topic Recommendations
After each answer, see 2 suggested follow-up topics:
- "Mainnet" → suggests "KYC" and "KYB" next
- "KYC" → suggests "KYB", "App Studio", or "Ecosystem"
- "Nodes" → suggests "Roadmap" or "App Studio"

One tap jumps to asking about the suggested topic.

### 🌐 Bilingual Support
- Switch between English and Vietnamese anytime
- All responses and recommendations adapt to your language
- Topic recommendations use your preferred language

## Pro Tips

1. **Ask specific questions** — More detail helps the advisor target better teaching material
2. **Follow the recommendations** — They guide you on a learning path from basic to advanced
3. **Ask "why"** — The advisor excels at explaining impact and importance
4. **Compare concepts** — Ask about KYC vs KYB or Mainnet vs current network
5. **Explore the roadmap** — Ask what's next for Pi Network to get forward-looking insights

## What It Won't Do

- ✗ Make price predictions (it's focused on understanding, not trading)
- ✗ Speculate about uncovered topics (it will clearly say "not in official sources")
- ✗ Use outside crypto knowledge (only Pi Network content)
- ✗ Skip citing sources (every fact has attribution)

## Questions That Work Best

✅ "What is the Mainnet?"
✅ "Explain KYC for beginners"
✅ "Why does the roadmap matter?"
✅ "What happens after KYC is complete?"
✅ "How do Nodes work?"
✅ "Tell me about App Studio"

✅ "What's the difference between KYC and KYB?"

## Questions It Will Redirect

❌ "When will Pi hit $1?" → "Sorry, I focus on Pi Network education, not price prediction."
❌ "Is Bitcoin better than Pi?" → "I only discuss Pi Network. Ask me more about Pi!"
❌ "How do I set up a crypto wallet?" → "I focus on Pi Network specifically. Anything about Pi I can help with?"

## Feedback & Improvements

If the advisor's teaching style isn't clear enough:
- Use simpler language in your questions
- Ask for examples or analogies
- Request a beginner explanation specifically
- Break multi-part questions into separate asks

## Language Switch

Top-right of the app shows "EN" or "VI". Tap to switch. Advisor automatically responds in your chosen language from that point forward.
